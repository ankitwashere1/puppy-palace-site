Good questions

Don't see yours? Call (443) 348-3964 and we'll sort it out.

Do I need an appointment?

Not for the self-service wash. Just walk in during open hours. Full-service baths and professional grooming are by appointment, so book online or give us a call.

What should I bring?

Just your pup. Shampoo, brushes, combs, towels and pet-friendly dryers are all here, and our staff will show you how to use everything.

Do you take big dogs?

Absolutely. Our raised tubs fit dogs of every size, and full-service baths are priced from extra small all the way up to 71 lbs and over.

Do you require vaccinations?

Give us a call before your first full-service or grooming visit and we'll walk you through what we need.

Can I drop my dog off?

Yes. For a full-service wash you can wait in the shop or drop off and run errands. We'll take good care of them either way.

How does Sunday Funday work?

Every Sunday from 10 am to 2 pm, the self-service wash is $15 instead of $20. No coupon needed.

How much does grooming cost?

It depends on your dog's breed, size and coat. Call us with those details and we'll give you an exact price.

Can someone come to my house?

Our partners at Puppy Love Mobile Pet Grooming bring the grooming to your door.
