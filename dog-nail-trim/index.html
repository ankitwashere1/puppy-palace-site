Why regular trims matter

Overgrown nails change the way a dog stands and walks, and they can split or snag. Many dogs need a trim every few weeks, especially if they mostly walk on grass or carpet. A quick visit between baths keeps them comfortable.

Nervous or senior dogs

Lots of dogs don't love having their paws handled. Our team works calmly and gently, and we've trimmed plenty of wiggly puppies and senior pups.

Make it a spa day

Pair a trim with a self-service wash ($20) or a full-service bath from $45.

Questions people ask

How much is a dog nail trim?

$10 as a drop-in, or $8 when you add it to a self-service wash.

Do I need an appointment for a nail trim?

No. Walk in during open hours: Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday 9 am to 5:30 pm, and Sunday 10 am to 2 pm.

Yes. Squirmy and nervous pups are welcome, and our team works calmly and gently.

Yes. Every dog that comes in for a nail trim or any service must be up to date on their vaccinations.

Do you clean ears and brush teeth too?

Yes. Ear cleaning is $7 ($5 with a wash) and teeth brushing is $11.
