How a self-service wash works

Puppy Palace was Woodlawn's first self-service dog wash. You bring your dog, we supply everything else. Our staff will show you how to brush, bathe and dry like a pro, then you take it from there at your own pace.

  • Raised tubs, so there's no kneeling on a bathroom floor
  • Premium shampoo
  • Brushes, combs and towels
  • Pet-friendly dryers
  • We do the cleanup when you're done

No appointment is needed. Just walk in during open hours. If you'd rather use your own shampoo, that's fine too.

Questions people ask

Do I need an appointment for the self-service dog wash?

No. Just walk in during open hours: Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday 9 am to 5:30 pm, and Sunday 10 am to 2 pm.

How much does a self-service dog wash cost?

$20 per wash. No appointment needed.

What do I need to bring?

Just your dog. Shampoo, brushes, combs, towels and pet-friendly dryers are all here, and our staff will show you how to use everything.

Can I wash a big dog?

Yes. Our raised tubs fit dogs of every size.

Do dogs need a rabies vaccination?

Yes. Every dog that comes in for a wash or any service must be up to date on their rabies vaccination.

Can I add a nail trim after my wash?

Yes. Add-ons cost less with a wash: nail trim $12, ear cleaning $10, teeth brushing $14, or all three as The Complete Pup for $24.
